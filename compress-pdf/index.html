PDF compressor

Re-save your PDF with a compact, modern structure to strip out wasted space. Honest savings, in your browser, with nothing uploaded.

Drop a PDF here or click to browse

Best results come from PDFs produced by scanners and office software that carry bloated structure.

Private by design: your PDF is processed in your browser only. It is never uploaded, so sensitive documents stay sensitive.

What this tool does — honestly

Many PDFs carry more weight than they need: uncompressed internal structure, duplicated objects and leftover data from the software that wrote them. This tool loads your PDF in the browser and re-saves it using compressed object streams, which packs the document's structure far more efficiently. For PDFs written by older scanners and office suites, that alone can trim a noticeable amount — sometimes 5–30%.

Here is what it does not do, because we would rather be straight with you: it does not shrink the images inside your PDF. If your file is large because it contains high-resolution scans or photos, re-saving the structure will only help a little, and no honest in-browser tool can rebuild those images at a lower resolution without changing how the pages look. For a dramatically smaller file in that case, the practical routes are to extract only the pages you need, or to re-create the PDF from smaller source images using our image compressor.

When this tool helps most

  • PDFs exported by older versions of Word, scanners and print-to-PDF drivers.
  • Documents that have been edited and re-saved many times, accumulating bloat.
  • Files that are just over an email or upload limit, where a modest saving is all you need.

The tool always shows you the before and after sizes before you download, so you can see exactly what you saved — and keep your original if the saving is not worth it.

PDF compressor — frequently asked questions

How much smaller will my PDF get?

It depends entirely on how the file was made. Structurally bloated PDFs often shrink by 5–30%. PDFs dominated by large images may barely change, and the tool will show you the true figures before you download anything.

Will the quality of my document change?

No. The pages, text and images are preserved exactly; only the internal packaging of the file is made more efficient. Nothing is downscaled or re-encoded.

Why do other sites promise 90% reductions?

Those services usually downscale the images inside your PDF on their servers, which changes quality — and requires you to upload the document. We chose the private route: honest structural optimisation on your own device.

Is my PDF uploaded?

Never. The optimisation runs in your browser using a PDF library loaded into the page.
