Contact us

Questions about a tool, a correction to a guide, or a suggestion for something we should build? We would like to hear from you.

The simplest way to reach us is by email at hello@snapfixy.com. Or use the form below: it prepares a message and opens it in your own email app, so you stay in control of what is sent — this page itself stores nothing.

Pressing the button opens your email programme with the message addressed to hello@snapfixy.com. Nothing is sent until you press send there.

Before you write

  • A tool did not work? Tell us which tool, which browser, and roughly what file you used (type and size — never send the file itself). Most problems are browser or memory related, and the detail helps us fix them.
  • Your file is never needed: because the tools run on your device, we cannot look at your file, recover it, or see what went wrong with it. Your original is always untouched, so you can simply try again.
  • Privacy questions are covered in our Privacy Policy; anything it does not answer, ask us here.

We aim to reply within two working days. SnapFixy is run by a small team (see About Us), so replies come from a person, not a ticket system.
