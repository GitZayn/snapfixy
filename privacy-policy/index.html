Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use SnapFixy — starting with the most important point.

Your files are never uploaded

Every SnapFixy tool runs entirely in your web browser. When you choose a photo or document, your browser opens it on your own device, processes it there, and offers you the result as a download. Your files are not transmitted to SnapFixy, are not stored by us, and are never seen by us. When you close or refresh the page, anything the tool was holding in memory is discarded by your browser.

Information we do and do not collect

There are no user accounts on SnapFixy, and we do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details in order to use the tools. We do not operate a database of visitors. Like almost every website, our hosting provider may record standard technical logs (such as the page requested, the time, and the type of browser) for security and reliability; these logs are not used to identify individual visitors and are not combined with your files, which we never receive in the first place.

If you contact us by email, we will receive the details you include in your message and will use them only to reply to you.

Cookies

SnapFixy itself sets no tracking cookies of its own. However, third-party services used by the site — in particular advertising — may set cookies in your browser, as described in the next section. Your browser also caches the tools' software (including the background remover's AI model) so pages load faster on later visits; that cache is stored on your device, is under your control, and contains no information about your files. The background remover's AI model files are downloaded from the IMG.LY content delivery network (via jsDelivr) the first time you use that tool; downloading the model sends no information about your files.

Advertising and cookies

SnapFixy is funded by advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices (for visitors in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu). Opting out does not remove advertising altogether; it means the ads you see will not be personalised using those cookies. None of this affects the tools themselves or your files, which never leave your device regardless of advertising.

Children's privacy

SnapFixy is a general-purpose tools website and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age, through the tools.

Links to other websites

Our pages may link to other websites, such as resources referenced in blog articles. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites, and we encourage you to read their own policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes to how files are handled would change the very nature of SnapFixy, and we would say so plainly here.

Contact

Questions about this policy are welcome via our contact page or by email at info@zee-developers.co.uk.
