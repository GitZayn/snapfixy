About SnapFixy

A small, independent tools site built on one stubborn idea: your files should never have to leave your device just to be edited.

What SnapFixy is

SnapFixy is a collection of free online tools for everyday image and document jobs: removing a photo background, compressing an image so it fits an upload limit, converting between PNG, JPG and WebP, resizing to exact dimensions, and merging, splitting or optimising PDFs. Each tool is a single, focused page that does one job well, without accounts, installs or watermarks.

Who runs it

SnapFixy is run by the small team behind Zee Developers, a UK-facing web design and development studio founded in 2020. We build websites for small businesses for a living, and SnapFixy grew out of the file-wrangling jobs our clients kept asking about — the marketplace photo that needs a clean background, the PDF that is too big to email, the form that will only accept a JPG. Rather than answering the same questions forever, we built the tools.

Our privacy-first approach

Most online tools work by uploading your file to a server. That means your photos and documents travel across the internet, sit on someone else's computer, and are covered by promises you have to take on trust. Modern browsers can now do the same work locally, so we built SnapFixy the other way round: the software comes to your files, not the other way round. When you use a SnapFixy tool, your browser loads the processing code, opens your file on your own device, does the job, and gives you the result. Nothing is uploaded, so there is no copy of your file on our side to protect, lose or misuse. You can read the detail in our Privacy Policy.

How the site is paid for

The tools are free to use. Running the site is funded by advertising displayed alongside the tools, which is why you may see ads on our pages. Advertising never changes how a tool behaves, and your files are just as private whether an ad is showing or not.
