Frequently asked questions

Are the tools really free?

Yes. Every tool on SnapFixy is free to use, with no watermarks on your downloads and no sign-up. The site is supported by advertising, which is why you may see ads alongside the tools — but the tools themselves cost nothing.

How can the tools work without uploading my files?

Modern browsers are powerful enough to run image processing, AI models and PDF libraries directly on your device. SnapFixy loads that software into your browser once, and your files are processed there. Because the file never travels over the internet, the tool also tends to feel fast — there is no upload or download queue.

Is there a file-size limit?

We do not impose one. The practical limit is your device's memory: very large images or PDFs with hundreds of pages will take longer and may strain an older phone. If a job struggles, try a smaller file or a desktop browser.

Which browsers are supported?

Any current browser — Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on desktop, and modern mobile browsers. The background remover works best in an up-to-date desktop browser, because it runs a small AI model on your device.

Do you keep a copy of anything I process?

No. Your files are never sent to us, so there is nothing for us to keep. You can read exactly how the site handles data in our Privacy Policy.
